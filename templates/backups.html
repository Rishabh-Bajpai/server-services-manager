Local-disk backups via systemd timers. Each job runs as a oneshot service (tar / mysqldump / pg_dump) on its schedule and prunes old archives beyond the retention count.
How to restore from an archive

Archives are stored in ~/.server-services-manager/backups/<job-name>/.

Directory (tar): tar -xzf <archive>.tar.gz -C /target/restore/path

MySQL: zcat <archive>.sql.gz | mysql -u root -p <database>

PostgreSQL: zcat <archive>.sql.gz | psql -U postgres <database>
